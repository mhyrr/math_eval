## Statement

> For a simple connected graph G, the size f(G) of a largest induced forest
> satisfies f(G) ≥ residue(G) + ⌈diam(G)/3⌉, where residue(G) is the
> Havel–Hakimi residue and diam(G) the diameter.

The statement file cites Favaron–Mahéo–Saclé (1991) for the residue
invariant.

## The certificate

A **no** is one connected graph; f(G) exact for small orders, residue and
diameter polynomial. Same sweep machinery as [wow2-059](wow2-059.md).

## Before an attempt

Same as [wow2-059](wow2-059.md): check DeLaVina's status page and citing
literature first, then run the class-level sweep. Long-diameter graphs
(paths with decorations) stress this inequality's second term and are the
natural targeted family after the exhaustive range.
