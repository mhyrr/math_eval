## Statement

> For a simple connected graph G, the size f(G) of a largest induced forest
> satisfies f(G) ≥ residue(G) + ⌈diam(G)/3⌉, where residue(G) is the
> Havel–Hakimi residue and diam(G) the diameter.

The statement file cites Favaron–Mahéo–Saclé (1991) for the residue
invariant.

## The certificate

A **no** is one connected graph; f(G) exact for small orders, residue and
diameter polynomial. Same sweep machinery as [wow2-059](wow2-059.md).

## Attempt of 2026-07-26

The exact class-level sweeper is now implemented in [`wow2/`](wow2/). No
counterexample was found: every connected unlabeled graph through order 9,
360,267 path-of-gadgets constructions through order 20, targeted extensions
of equality cases, and about 7.5 million edge-toggle proposals all failed to
cross the boundary. Every connected candidate was evaluated exactly. The
maximum observed value was \(\Phi(G)=0\).

The bounded null result leaves the conjecture open. Counts, boundary graphs,
independent-oracle checks, and reproduction commands are in
[`wow2/RESULT-061.md`](wow2/RESULT-061.md).

## Proof attack of 2026-07-27

The null search led to a direct sufficient certificate. If \(G\) has a
distance-three packing \(S\) of order \(\lceil d(G)/3\rceil\) such that
\(\alpha(G-S)\ge r(G)\), an independent \(r(G)\)-set in \(G-S\), together
with \(S\), induces a star forest of the required order.

The remaining structural lemma asserts that such an \(S\) always exists. A
stronger, residue-native form says that the maximum-degree choices in the
Griggs–Kleitman `MAX` algorithm can be broken so that its first
\(n-r(G)\) deleted vertices contain the packing. Both formulations hold on
all 2,592,586 evaluated records (880,890 with diameter at least four), but
neither is proved.

There is also a direct inductive route. Deleting any maximum-degree vertex
loses at most one unit from the componentwise target bound. It would therefore
suffice to prove that some vertex loses no unit; this deletion lemma also held
on every evaluated record.

of the reductions, exact counts, failed strengthenings, references, and
reproduction commands, is
